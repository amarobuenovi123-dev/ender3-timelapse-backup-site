Ender3 Timelapse Backup

Personal Google Drive backup integration for timelapse recordings from a Creality Ender 3 V3 SE 3D printer.

Purpose

This application uses Rclone and Google OAuth to securely upload timelapse videos from a personally managed Orange Pi to the owner's Google Drive account.

It is intended for personal use only. It does not offer public registration, advertising, or commercial services.
